# Japan Watchface
Car and Japan themed data-heavy watchface developed for the Amazfit T-Rex 3, Amazfit T-rex 3 pro (48mm) and Amazfit Balance 2

## Tools used
- Zepp OS SDK v3.5
- Zeus CLI
## Watchface features
-12 fps animation of cars going around Japan  
-Clock (hours, minutes and seconds) including a countdown shortcut  
-Calorie widget with a progressive bar, number and a shortcut  
-Batterie widget with a progressive bar, number and shortcut  
-Weather widgets featuring current, min and max temperatures, city name, atmospheric pressure in hPa, wind force, weather image, bigger segmented UVI circle, smaller segmented umidity circle and city name, with a weather shortcut  
-Distance walked with a shortcut  
-Date of the month, date of the week and month  
-A "velocimeter" based on Forza Horizon 6's flat velocimeter, with the biocharge as the gears (with a shortcut) and BPM as the speed (with a shortcut)  
-Simple AoD display with hours, minutes, calories and battery
